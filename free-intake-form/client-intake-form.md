# Client Intake Form

**Manifest Miracle Coaching** · Free template for new 1:1 coaches

You can paste this into Google Docs or Google Forms, or print the PDF.

## How to use this (for the coach)

1. Make a copy in Google Docs, or create a Google Form with these questions.
2. In Google Forms, turn on “Collect email addresses.”
3. Share the form link in your welcome email, before Session 1.
4. Keep responses in a private Drive folder. Never share a client’s answers publicly.

You are not collecting a medical history. Keep questions respectful. Store responses securely.

---

## Section A — Basics

1. Full name
2. Preferred name / pronouns
3. Email
4. Phone (optional)
5. Time zone
6. How did you find me?

## Section B — Goals

7. What do you most want to change or create in the next 90 days?
8. What would “success” look like after our work together? (Be specific.)
9. What’s already working that you want to keep?
10. On a scale of 1–10, how ready are you to take action weekly?

## Section C — Context (optional; the client may skip)

11. Anything I should know so I can support you well? (life transitions, constraints, preferences)
12. Are you currently working with a therapist, counselor, or other professional?
    - Yes
    - No
    - Prefer not to say
13. Coaching is not therapy. If you need clinical support, please continue (or seek) licensed care. Understood?
    - Yes

## Section D — Logistics

14. Preferred session days/times
15. Preferred format: Zoom / Phone / Other
16. Emergency contact name + phone (optional; for no-show wellness checks only if you choose to use it)
17. Anything else you want me to know before Session 1?
